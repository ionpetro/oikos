# Foundations author record

Completed u01–u06: 24 lessons, 168 original semantic items, and 24 held-back transfers. Every lesson has seven items: a taught principle with two authored checks, a term, two ordinary applications, a transfer application, and two additional discrimination, calculation, sequencing, matching or misconception activities. Every fourth lesson contains explicit boundary scenarios.

## Objectives and coverage

### u01: What are you giving up?

- **u01l1 — Which options are actually open?:** Distinguish feasible alternatives from wishes and already-excluded options. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, steps. Transfer: u01-l1-transfer.
- **u01l2 — Find the next-best alternative:** Identify the best feasible alternative forgone, using net rather than gross pay where appropriate. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, trap. Transfer: u01-l2-transfer.
- **u01l3 — Count time and money once:** Build a transparent direct-plus-forgone comparison without double counting an already net alternative. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, pairs, num, argue. Transfer: u01-l3-transfer.
- **u01l4 — When the constraint changes:** Update a comparison after a relevant constraint changes and distinguish feasibility from preference. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, trap. Transfer: u01-l4-transfer.

### u02: What changes with one more?

- **u02l1 — Totals are not additions:** Calculate a change from two totals and distinguish it from an average. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, num. Transfer: u02-l1-transfer.
- **u02l2 — Choose how many, under stated conditions:** Use an incremental schedule and check all feasible quantities when identifying the best total. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, trap. Transfer: u02-l2-transfer.
- **u02l3 — Leave irrecoverable costs in the past:** Separate sunk expenditure from the costs and benefits changed by a live decision. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, steps. Transfer: u02-l3-transfer.
- **u02l4 — Refunds, thresholds and bundles:** Compare complete feasible packages when refunds or complementary activities alter marginal reasoning. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, trap. Transfer: u02-l4-transfer.

### u03: What does the system reward?

- **u03l1 — Read the reward mechanism:** Identify what a reward formula changes without claiming it determines behavior. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, pairs, argue. Transfer: u03-l1-transfer.
- **u03l2 — Notice the work the measure misses:** Distinguish a proxy from a goal and identify a task-allocation risk under explicit conditions. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, sort. Transfer: u03-l2-transfer.
- **u03l3 — A penalty can change the meaning:** Interpret a bounded finding without turning its mechanism into a universal prediction. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, trap, sort, argue. Transfer: u03-l3-transfer.
- **u03l4 — Test the system before scaling:** Distinguish a mechanism hypothesis from evidence and preserve measurement limits in a pilot. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, steps, trap. Transfer: u03-l4-transfer.

### u04: How do markets fit together?

- **u04l1 — Read a demand schedule:** Distinguish a price–quantity relationship from need, preference and a demand shift. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, trap, argue. Transfer: u04-l1-transfer.
- **u04l2 — Find a model’s clearing point:** Use demand and supply schedules to identify an equilibrium and a shortage at a stated price. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, pairs. Transfer: u04-l2-transfer.
- **u04l3 — Separate shifts from stories:** Predict a directional change only under specified shifts and recognize an indeterminate price when two curves change. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, trap. Transfer: u04-l3-transfer.
- **u04l4 — Know when the benchmark ends:** Recognize omitted market structure, information, access and ethical criteria before applying a competitive benchmark. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, trap, argue. Transfer: u04-l4-transfer.

### u05: Who responds, and who pays?

- **u05l1 — Measure responsiveness:** Calculate a specified price elasticity and distinguish elastic, inelastic and zero response. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, sort, argue. Transfer: u05-l1-transfer.
- **u05l2 — Separate revenue from profit:** Calculate revenue and relevant cost changes before inferring a profit change. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, pairs, num. Transfer: u05-l2-transfer.
- **u05l3 — Follow the tax wedge:** Calculate buyer price, seller receipt, tax revenue and incidence relative to a common no-tax baseline. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, sort. Transfer: u05-l3-transfer.
- **u05l4 — Respect incidence assumptions:** State the conditions behind incidence predictions and distinguish them from distributional or policy judgments. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, trap, sort, argue. Transfer: u05-l4-transfer.

### u06: Who else feels the effects?

- **u06l1 — Look beyond the transaction:** Identify a third-party effect and distinguish it from a direct cost or benefit of the transaction. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, sort, pairs. Transfer: u06-l1-transfer.
- **u06l2 — Expand the margin carefully:** Include specified external costs in a marginal comparison without treating the chosen valuation as universal. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, trap. Transfer: u06-l2-transfer.
- **u06l3 — Compare ways to respond:** Compare correction, prevention and compensation without treating any instrument as automatically sufficient. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, steps, sort, argue. Transfer: u06-l3-transfer.
- **u06l4 — Do not hide the people in totals:** Distinguish a bounded cost-benefit result from consent, compensation, complete harm measurement and distribution. Kinds: rule, term, hypo, num, trap, argue. Transfer: u06-l4-transfer.

## Provenance and scope

F15 is used to separate calculated totals from fairness. Every hypothetical person, institution, amount, schedule and result is authored fiction unless identified as a reported study.

The day-care finding is described as a bounded study of ten centers, with six receiving the particular fine; proposed mechanisms and extrapolations are distinguished from its observed result. No effect size or universal behavioral forecast is invented. Market, elasticity, incidence and social-cost relationships carry explicit model conditions. Financial figures are hypothetical learning exercises rather than recommendations. Aggregation criteria are stated; neither money totals nor game scores rank human worth, confer consent or guarantee a policy outcome.
