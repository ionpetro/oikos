## Method

Read authored teaching, examples, checks, scenario keys and every alternative explanation; check model assumptions, empirical scope, normative criteria, deciding-fact spans and prerequisite order. Recompute all numerical items. Inspect generated alternatives, retrieval variants and boundary cases over 40 seeds through the inherited test family. A structural pass is not proof of a defensible content key.

The ethical criterion is quality of reasoning under the stated facts, not agreement with a preferred policy or a moral-person score. Conditional model results, evidence and evaluative choices must remain distinct. The separate actual-model audit is in `review-models.md`.
